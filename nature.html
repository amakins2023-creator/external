Forest Adventure

The Forest is one of the backbones of earth with all the tress and all it's inhabitants.

River Journey

Rivers provide water for people, plants, and animals. They also make beautiful natural landscapes.

Mountain View

Mountains are tall landforms that give us amazing views and are home to many unique plants and animals.

Wildlife Zone

The Wildlife Zone is occupied by te mostly predators and their predators we study the wildlife zone more than the others because it gives more information about the food chain.

Nature Explorer Checklist

  • Observe plants carefully
  • Do not disturb animals
  • Keep the environment clean
  • Carry a reuseable water bottle
  • Do not feed animals
  • No sharp objects (Knives)

Nature Tip: Protecting nature means protecting our future.
